# SnapCollage

A free, private photo-collage app you can install on your phone (PWA). Your photos never leave your device: everything is processed in your browser, with no uploads, accounts, analytics or trackers, and a strict Content-Security-Policy.

Live: https://davidsandyofficial.github.io/snapcollage/
